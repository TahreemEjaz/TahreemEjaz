OG Preview Image Generator

This tool generates the 1200 × 630 Open Graph / social card image for tahreem-fatima.dev using HTML Canvas. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts — wait ~1 second before downloading.

How to use

  1. Wait ~1 second for fonts to load, then click Download og-preview.png.
  2. Place og-preview.png in your portfolio's root folder (same level as porfolio-tahreem.html).
  3. Deploy — the meta tags already reference /og-preview.png at your domain.
  4. Test your card at opengraph.xyz or cards-dev.twitter.com/validator.
